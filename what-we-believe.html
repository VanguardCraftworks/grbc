What We Believe

The Second London Baptist Confession of Faith

We believe the Bible is the inspired, inerrant, and authoritative Word of God, and the sole basis for any statement of faith. We adopt the Second London Baptist Confession of Faith of 1689 as a faithful and historic expression of that faith — not as a rival to Scripture, but as an assistance in controversy and a confirmation in belief.
